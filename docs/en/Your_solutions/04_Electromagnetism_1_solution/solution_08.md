### 8. Lorentz Force

**Given:**
- Charge, \( q = 2 \times 10^{-19} \, \text{C} \)
- Magnetic field, \( B = 0.5 \, \text{T} \)
- Velocity, \( v = 1 \times 10^{6} \, \text{m/s} \)
- Angle, \( \theta = 90^\circ \)

**Formula:**
\[
F = qvB \sin\theta
\]

**Calculation:**
\[
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(1 \times 10^{6})(0.5)(\sin 90^\circ)
\]
\[
F = (2 \times 10^{-19})(1 \times 10^{6})(0.5)(1)
\]
\[
F = 1 \times 10^{-13} \, \text{N}
\]

**Final Answer:**
\[
\boxed{1 \times 10^{-13} \, \text{N}}
\]
